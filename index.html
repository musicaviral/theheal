THE PRESENTATION

The interest behind the conversation

When changes in energy, circulation or intimate life start to raise concerns, it's natural to look for answers. Gelatin has gained space in these conversations for being a well-known food associated with collagen.

However, this does not mean that eating gelatin treats erectile dysfunction, increases penis size or replaces medical treatments. Claims like these require reliable clinical studies, and quick fixes deserve caution.

THE MAIN POINT

Intimate health has many possible causes. An individual evaluation helps you understand what is happening and which care actually makes sense.

A safer path

If you've noticed persistent changes, talk to a healthcare professional. Circulation issues, medications, sleep and other conditions can influence sexual well-being — and they deserve proper attention, without embarrassment.
